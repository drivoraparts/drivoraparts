# Supplier acquisition package (prepared 2026-11-19)

For the owner to send and sign. Nothing here has been sent, applied for or agreed.
Contact details come from the suppliers' public pages as indexed on 2026-11-19; confirm
them by phone before relying on them. "Ask" means the term is not published and must be
obtained from the supplier.

## What you need before applying

Every wholesale channel below asks for some or all of:
- Legal business name, EIN, business address and phone.
- **California seller's permit** (CDTFA) and a **resale certificate** (CDTFA-230) so parts
  are bought tax-free for resale.
- City business license.
- Credit application with bank and trade references (or a card on file for prepaid terms).
- For LKQ: a signed NDA, data usage agreement and tax certification forms.

## Priority 1: Toyota dealer wholesale accounts (genuine new and Genuine Reman parts)

Toyota does not publish a reseller or drop-ship program. Wholesale is run by individual
dealers, who set their own pricing, and through Toyota's dealer-fulfilled ordering for shops
(Toyota Parts Center Online / MyPARTS, RepairLink, Microcat PartsBridge). Dealers can ship
selected orders straight from Toyota's parts distribution centers under the dealer-only PDC
Direct Fulfillment program, so drop-shipping is possible only if a dealer agrees to do it.

## Questions to put to every supplier

1. Account type for an online reseller; documents required; credit terms or prepay.
2. Pricing basis: percent off MSRP, cost-plus, or tiered by volume; any category exclusions
   (hybrid batteries, reman, collision).
3. Drop-ship to our customers in the 48 contiguous states + DC; blind shipping (our name on the
   packing slip); handling fee per order.
4. Freight: parcel and LTL rates or carrier accounts; oversize charges; hazmat (HV batteries).
5. Stock visibility: inventory feed or API; typical lead time from Toyota PDC.
6. Returns: restocking fee, return window, electrical/special-order exclusions, core returns.
7. Warranty handling for parts installed by independent shops.
8. Images and data: may we use your product photos and descriptions on our site and Google
   Shopping? In writing.
9. Fraud and payment: who carries chargeback risk on drop-shipped orders.
