## Profitability

No supplier cost is on file for any of the 50 records, so profitability cannot be established for any of them and **every** target price is flagged for review rather than presented as a selling price. Once the owner supplies a supplier cost and a minimum margin (`SUPPLIER_COST` and `MIN_MARGIN` in `scripts/toyota_expansion_pricing.py`), each price is checked at 5% under; if that misses the margin it moves to 3% under; if 3% under still misses, the part is flagged *not profitable at a competitive price* and is not listed by undercutting. Margin per unit = selling price - supplier cost - payment processing fee - packaging - outbound shipping (free on US parcel orders under $1,000).
