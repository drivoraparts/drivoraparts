## Outstanding verification issues (all listings)

1. Supplier and fulfilment: no supplier account, wholesale price list or drop-ship agreement exists. Dealer and retailer pages are price references, not suppliers.
2. Inventory: not verified. Listings show the storefront's standard "In stock" wording and Merchant availability, kept as-is at the owner's instruction (2026-11-19).
3. Image rights: Toyota catalog photos (epc-images.toyota.com) are used at the owner's instruction; commercial licence not established.
4. Cost and profitability: unknown. Prices sit about 5% below the lowest retail price, so buying at retail would lose money; a wholesale/dealer-cost source is required.
5. Photo coverage: Toyota publishes photos mainly for 2019+ platforms; most 3rd-gen Tacoma, 5th-gen 4Runner, older Camry/Corolla parts, TRD/accessory parts and many drivetrain parts have none, which caps how many products can qualify under the current image rule.
